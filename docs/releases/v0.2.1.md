Fixes the free space shown for drives.

## Download
- **Moon-Explorer-Setup-0.2.1.exe**: installer. Installs per user, no admin rights needed. It updates 0.2.0 in place.
- **Moon-Explorer-0.2.1-win-x64.zip**: portable version.

The builds are not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen warns the first time you start them. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway".

## Fixed
- **Drive space** now matches Windows Explorer, e.g. "145 GB free of 1.81 TB". Before, it could show far too little free space (it was read only once, at start) and rounded the size to "2 TB". (#9)

## New
- **Refresh drives**: a refresh button next to the drives in the sidebar and in This PC, and F5 in This PC. Free space is also checked again when the window comes back to the front, once a minute, and after copying, moving or deleting. (#9)
